# Section Playbook

Use the subsection that matches the task.

## Literature Review

Goal:

- Organize the literature into strands, disagreements, and open questions.

Rewrite priorities:

- Prefer synthesis and comparison over name-heavy listing.
- Replace vague phrases such as "many scholars believe" with concrete positioning when the source text supports it.
- Do not let the literature review drift into introduction voice with contribution claims or paper-structure framing.
- Do not let the literature review drift into conclusion voice with policy implications or future-research wrap-ups.

## Mechanism Discussion

Goal:

- Explain the mechanism clearly before making a directional claim.

Rewrite priorities:

- Define the mechanism before interpreting it.
- Avoid rhetorical buildup and broad framing.

## Variables, Data, And Model

Goal:

- Define variables, data sources, sample filters, and model choices clearly.

Rewrite priorities:

- Put the object first: variable, proxy, sample, source, filter, or model.
- Preserve notation, transformations, fixed effects, and clustering details.

## Regression Interpretation

Goal:

- Report sign, significance, magnitude, and meaning accurately.

Rewrite priorities:

- Move in the order: result, magnitude, interpretation.
- Keep statements proportional to the evidence.

## Robustness Checks

Goal:

- Explain what changed, what remained stable, and what the robustness exercise implies.

Rewrite priorities:

- Distinguish between robust, weaker, and mixed results.
- Do not oversell robustness.
