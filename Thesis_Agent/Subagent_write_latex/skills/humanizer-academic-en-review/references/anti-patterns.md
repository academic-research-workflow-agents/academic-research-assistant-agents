# Academic Anti-Patterns

## 1. Inflated Significance

Watch for:

- "pivotal"
- "transformative"
- "crucial"

Better move:

- State the actual research problem, comparison, or disagreement.

## 2. Vague Attribution

Watch for:

- "studies have shown"
- "scholars argue"

Better move:

- Keep the strand precise or name the actual disagreement when the source supports it.

## 3. Empty Contribution Claims

Watch for:

- "fills the gap"
- "enriches the literature"

Better move:

- Specify what is added: setting, mechanism, proxy, or comparison dimension.

## 4. Introduction Voice Leakage

Watch for:

- "this paper contributes"
- "the remainder of this paper"

Better move:

- Return to the literature itself instead of framing the review around the paper.

## 5. Conclusion Voice Leakage

Watch for:

- "policy implications"
- "future research"

Better move:

- End on unresolved debates or research gaps rather than on a conclusion-style wrap-up.

## 6. Blurry Humanizing

Watch for:

- revisions that sound smoother only because they remove definitions, disagreement, or identification detail

Better move:

- Clean the style while preserving precision and argumentative structure.
